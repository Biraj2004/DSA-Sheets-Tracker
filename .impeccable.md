# Impeccable Design Context: DSA Tracker

## Design Context

### Users
- **Who they are**: Students, software engineers, and job seekers preparing for technical interviews and coding rounds at tech companies (FAANG, tier-1 startups, enterprise).
- **Their context**: High-stress preparation, often coding for hours late at night or early morning. They need clarity, zero distractions, instant feedback, and total confidence that their progress will never vanish.
- **The job to be done**: Systematically work through curated DSA sheets (Striver A2Z, Love Babbar 450, NeetCode 150/250, Apna College), track completion, record personal notes and complexities, and review problems on a spaced repetition schedule.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice and Tone**: Calm, disciplined, trustworthy, precise.
- **3-Word Personality**: Focused, Crafted, Dependable.
- **Emotional Goals**: Gives the learner clarity, sense of mastery, and peace of mind. Reduces cognitive fatigue.
- **Explicit Anti-References**:
  - NO AI-slop neon glows (cyan `#00e5ff` / purple `#7c5cfc` gradients against pitch black).
  - NO emojis anywhere in the interface or headings (no `🧮`, `🔃`, `🪟`, `⚡`, `⭐`, `📝` - use clean Lucide SVG icons only).
  - NO crowded, unpadded tables with tiny touch targets.
  - NO distracting third-party advertisements or invasive banners.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Direction**: Editorial precision, clean engineering, refined Scandinavian minimalism.
- **Theme**: Seamless Light and Dark modes.
  - Dark mode: Deep neutral slate-tinted zinc/slate backgrounds (not pure #000000), soft border delineations, subtle contrast.
  - Light mode: Crisp warm-white canvas with gentle slate borders and deep charcoal typography.
  - Accent: High-contrast single primary accent (e.g. indigo / electric slate) with dedicated, WCAG AA-accessible semantic status tokens (Solved Emerald, Tried Amber, Revise Rose, Star Gold).
- **Typography**:
  - Primary Body & Headings: **Inter** (clean modular type scale, fluid optical sizes, 400/500/600 weights).
  - Metrics, Numbers & Code: **JetBrains Mono** with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter in counters and progress badges.
- **Icons**:
  - **Lucide Icons** exclusively, styled with consistent 1.75px stroke width and standardized bounding boxes (16px / 20px). Zero emojis.

### Step-Wise Arrangement & Structure
- Each sheet maintains its creator's original authentic hierarchy and step progression.
- Content is broken down cleanly into collapsible, well-spaced step cards with clear section headers, progress indicators (`14 / 63`), and estimated completion metrics.
- Unrelated questions (like 30 Days of JS or advanced Math) are never dumped into introductory steps.

### Design Principles
1. **Content First, Distraction Zero**: The problem and learning path are the hero. Everything else recedes into the background until needed.
2. **Deterministic & Honest**: Never show "Saved" until data is safely written to disk. Never show fabricated counts or mislabeled platform badges.
3. **Ergonomic Keyboard & Touch Navigation**: First-class keyboard shortcuts (`/`, `j`, `k`, `x`, `s`, `n`, `?`) paired with generous 44px touch targets on mobile.
4. **Spatial Rhythm & Restraint**: Adhere to a strict 4px grid. Varied spacing creates hierarchy without nesting endless boxes within boxes.
